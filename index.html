About Glint

We started Glint because most agency work looks the same.

Glint is a small studio of strategists, writers, and designers who got tired of watching good products launch with forgettable marketing. We built the studio around one rule: every deliverable has to earn attention on its own, without relying on a client's existing audience.

We work closely with founders and internal marketing teams — never as a black box, always as an embedded partner who understands the business behind the brand.
